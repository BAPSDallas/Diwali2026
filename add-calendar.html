Google Calendar adds one event at a time. Tap Add for each, then come back here.

    How to add the events

    All times are Central Time. You get two reminders for each event: one week before and one day before.

    Select your calendar.

    1. Touch Add to Apple Calendar.
    2. Open the file that your device downloads.
    3. Touch Add All in the Calendar app.
    4. Make sure that the events are in your calendar.
    1. Touch Add to Google Calendar.
    2. Touch Add for the first event in the list.
    3. Touch Save in Google Calendar.
    4. Do step 2 and step 3 again for each event.
